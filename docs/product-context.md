# Product Context

## What is LOOP?

**LOOP** is an iPhone app for students and early builders in Saudi Arabia who want to **build real things with other people**: find a build partner, join a hackathon team, and actually start working together.

It is Arabic-first (written in a natural Saudi tone, not translated) with a full English mode.

## Origin

LOOP was shaped by a recurring gap:

- Students want to build, but finding the right partner is luck and a group-chat post
- Hackathon teams form in the last days, by whoever answers first, not by the roles the team needs
- Profiles don't say what someone can actually do or what they're looking for
- Even good matches die quietly because nobody knows what to do first

LOOP answers one question:

> How do we help people find the right person to build with — and make the first step of building happen?

## Design Philosophy

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Complete each other** | Matching pairs what you offer with what someone needs, and says why in one line |
| **Start, don't just match** | Every new partnership gets a first thing to build (the challenge dice) |
| **Structure for teams** | Open seats per role, join requests, capacity, and a team space with voice |
| **Members grow it** | Share links solve the sharer's own need ("we need a designer"), so growth comes from use |
| **Safe by default** | Content filter, report and block everywhere, a 24h review promise |
| **Measure the real thing** | Success is partnerships that start working, not sign-ups |

## Target Users

- **Builders** — looking for a partner or recruiting for a hackathon team
- **Learners** — want to learn by building with someone
- **Hackathon participants** — need the missing role before the weekend
- **Reviewers** — recruiters, PMs and TPMs evaluating product and systems thinking
