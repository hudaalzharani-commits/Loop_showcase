# Product Context

## What is LOOP?

**LOOP** is a mobile-first community platform for developers who want to **learn, build, and grow together** — not alone in tabs and Discord servers.

It connects learners, builders, and mentors around shared goals: study rooms, project partners, hackathon teams, portfolios, and in-app collaboration.

## Origin

LOOP was shaped by a recurring gap in developer communities:

- Learners want accountability and peers, but discovery is noisy
- Builders need teammates for hackathons and side projects, but matching is manual
- Mentors and founders want structured ways to help without drowning in DMs
- “Community” tools exist — but few are designed as a **product workflow** for learning + building

LOOP answers one question:

> How do we help developers find the right people, rooms, and teams — then actually collaborate?

## Design Philosophy

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Community as product** | Discovery, requests, rooms, and chat are one loop — not bolted-on social features |
| **Intent over browsing** | Users declare what they are looking for (study, build partner, hackathon team) |
| **Structured matching** | Requests have lifecycle, capacity, and clear accept / decline paths |
| **Builder-first identity** | Profiles, skills, GitHub portfolio, and active-team signals matter |
| **Operational clarity** | Support, admin, and founder-direct channels keep the platform runnable |

## Target Users

- **Learners** — looking for study accountability and peer energy
- **Builders** — recruiting or joining project / hackathon teammates
- **Mentors** — visible guides inside the same discovery fabric
- **Reviewers** — recruiters, TPMs, and PMs evaluating product + systems thinking
